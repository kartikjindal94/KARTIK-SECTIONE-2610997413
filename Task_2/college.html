5. College Terminology

Semester
A part of the academic year during which classes and assessments take place.
Lecture
A class in which a teacher explains a subject or topic to students.
Assignment
A task given to students to complete as part of their studies.
Attendance
A record of a student's presence in classes or other required sessions.
Examination
A test used to evaluate a student's knowledge or understanding of a subject.
Credit
A unit that represents the academic value or workload of a course.
